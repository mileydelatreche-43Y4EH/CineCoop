CineCoop

Privacy policy

Last updated: May 2026

CineCoop is a Chrome extension that enables synchronized watch parties on streaming platforms.

Data we collect

  • Discord profile (optional): user ID, username, and avatar, only if you sign in for chat.
  • Local data: session preferences and chat language, stored in your browser.

Video sync

Synchronized playback uses a peer-to-peer connection (PeerJS) between participants. CineCoop does not record the video stream from the platform.

Authentication server

Discord sign-in goes through our secure server (HTTPS) for OAuth. We do not sell your data.
