Quick guide

Which browsers does CineCoop support?

Google Chrome and Chromium-based browsers.

How many people can join?

Up to 4 people (1 host + 3 guests).

Do I need a Discord account?

Not to watch. Optional for chat, GIFs, voice, and video.

How do I invite friends?

Copy the invite link from the CineCoop panel.

Is CineCoop safe?

Peer-to-peer sync. Secure Discord OAuth. Privacy policy.
